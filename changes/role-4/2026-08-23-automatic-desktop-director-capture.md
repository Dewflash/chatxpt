# Automatic Desktop Director capture setup

- **Type:** Added
- **Role:** Role 4
- **Issue/PR:** pending
- **Summary:** Profile & Defaults now lets the streamer choose automatic or manual Desktop Director setup. Automatic mode opens the already-linked desktop companion after a gameplay capture source connects. Legacy profiles resolve safely to Automatic, and the optional companion launch is isolated from gameplay capture and the public OBS overlay.
- **Integration impact:** Adds a saved profile preference consumed by Role 4 Gameplay Capture and a token-free `chatxpt://open` action handled by the Role 1 desktop companion. Missing legacy state or a failed custom-protocol handoff is contained and cannot tear down capture. The packaged companion exclusively owns the macOS scheme; source Electron runs cannot steal it.
