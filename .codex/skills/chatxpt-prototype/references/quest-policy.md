# Sidequest Policy

## Required output qualities

Return three options that differ in play pattern, not merely wording. Every option includes a short title, one unambiguous instruction, a duration or completion condition, difficulty, reward points, and a producer-only rationale tied to current signals.

## Adaptation rules

- Low health: avoid mandatory aggression or healing bans unless high intensity is allowed.
- Final circle: use short challenges that do not distract from essential communication.
- Teammate knocked: prioritize rescue, protection, or supportive narration over selfish looting.
- Bored audience during looting: favor performance, inventory, or movement constraints.
- Hype or chaos requests: increase spectacle without real-world risk or non-consensual team sabotage.
- Beginner-friendly profile: favor achievable, instructional, or comedic options.
- Competitive profile: favor measurable tactical goals; avoid forced role-play unless allowed.

## Rejection conditions

Reject and fall back when output ignores a streamer boundary, depends on unavailable mechanics, requests real money or dangerous acts, targets a person for humiliation, cannot be judged complete/failed, or duplicates another option's core action.

The producer is the final approval boundary before activation.
